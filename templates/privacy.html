Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 30, 2026 | Governing Jurisdiction: India

1. Commitment to Financial Confidentiality

Personal Finance Advisor Bot ("Application", "we", "us") is designed with strict data isolation standards. This Privacy Policy outlines our procedures regarding the collection, storage, and processing of personal cash flow records, budget limits, and financial metrics.

2. Data Collection and Scoping

We process only the data explicitly entered into the system by the user:

  • Account Authentication: Full name, verified email address, and cryptographically salted password hashes. Plaintext passwords are never retained.
  • Ledger Records: Income values, sources, categorized expenses, timestamps, and descriptive transaction tags in Indian Rupees (INR / ₹).
  • Planning Directives: Monthly category expenditure thresholds, emergency reserve targets, and savings goals.

3. Data Storage & Local Persistence

All user financial ledgers are persisted in an isolated SQLite relational database. Access to records is constrained strictly to the authenticated session owner through user ID relational foreign keys. We do not sell, rent, monetize, or broker personal financial data to third-party data aggregators or advertisers.

4. Google Gemini AI Diagnostics Integration

When you trigger the AI Advisor feature, the application transmits structured aggregate metrics (such as total monthly income, expense category sums, and budget limits) via the official Google Gemini API:

  • No government identification numbers, PAN details, bank account numbers, or debit card credentials are collected or transmitted.
  • Requests are sent over TLS 1.3 encrypted connections.
  • Queries processed through standard developer API endpoints are subject to Google Cloud and Gemini API confidentiality policies.

5. Security Controls

We employ standard defense-in-depth measures, including PBKDF2/scrypt password hashing with unique salts, secure HTTP-only cookies to prevent cross-site scripting (XSS) extraction, and SQL parameterization via SQLAlchemy to prevent SQL injection vulnerabilities.

6. User Control and Data Purging

Users maintain full sovereignty over their records. You may edit or delete individual income transactions, expenses, budget limits, and savings goals at any time via the application interface.

7. Amendments

We may update this policy as technical requirements evolve. Material modifications will be reflected by the Effective Date indicator at the top of this document.
